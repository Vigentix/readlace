- Publish a build of this code, modified or not, to the Chrome Web Store,

  or supports your product.
- Register domain names, store listings or social accounts that use the
  Readlace name for a product that is not the official one.

Before you publish a fork, replace the name in `public/_locales/*/messages.json`
and `wxt.config.ts`, the logo and icon files, `components/Mark.tsx` and the store
listing.
